## Editorial and affiliate rules

- Write in English with an entertaining, persuasive style and light humor.
- Commercial usefulness comes first, but the reader must receive a complete answer before the CTA.
- Attribute listings, booking pages and availability to GetYourGuide; attribute actual operation to the named activity provider.
- Never guarantee a vessel, meal, cabin, route, pickup, cancellation rule, price or inclusion that may change. Tell readers to verify the current GetYourGuide listing.
- Use only currently relevant cruise affiliate links/widgets, favouring credible mid-range or premium products. Do not insert unrelated attraction widgets.
- Show a visible affiliate disclosure and do not describe clicks as proven sales.
- Check mobile layout, image licences, internal links, canonical and sitemap before publishing.
